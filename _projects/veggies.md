## Connecting farmers with technology

![Veggies logo](/images/veggies-logo.png)

Veggies is an Android mobile app that uses machine learning to support farmers in their daily work. It includes:

- timely crop disease detection
- weather alerts
- a platform for selling agricultural produce
- crop suggestions based on season, location, and soil pH
